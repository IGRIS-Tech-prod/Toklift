Questions

Everything you're about to ask

Is it actually free?

Yes. All four packages are free. The tasks are how the network funds the lift — you follow two accounts and run a profile check, that's your entire cost.

Do I need a certain number of followers?

No. You can claim with 0 followers. Verification is granted to everyone who completes the tasks, which is exactly why it's worth doing now before it gets capped.

Why do you need my TikTok login?

So the allocation is attached to the correct account. Your username and password are used once to authorise the lift, then a 6-digit code confirms it's really you.

How fast will I see the followers?

Batches start within a few hours and complete inside 24 hours. You'll see it climb in stages — that's intentional, it looks more natural than one big spike.

Do I need to keep the account public?

Yes, keep it public for the 24h delivery window. Private accounts can't be attached to the network until they're open.

Is my allocation held?

Yes — your slot is reserved for 20 minutes once you start. If you leave mid-way you keep your progress, but the queue moves fast.
